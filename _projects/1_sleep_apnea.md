Obstructive sleep apnea is common and badly under-diagnosed, partly because the standard test, overnight polysomnography, is expensive and slow. This project, my M.Tech thesis, builds a deep learning framework that detects apnea events from single-lead ECG signals, a much cheaper and easier signal to record, as a step toward automated clinical screening.

**Tools:** Python, deep learning, biomedical signal processing.

**Code:** [github.com/srinivas50505/Automated-Sleep-Apnea-using-DL](https://github.com/srinivas50505/Automated-Sleep-Apnea-using-DL)
